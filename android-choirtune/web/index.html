Estimates pitches for Soprano (C4-C6), Alto (G3-D5), Tenor (C3-A4), and Bass (E2-E4). These are overlapping guide ranges, not rules about a singer's gender or voice type. SATB labels are inferred from pitch and continuity, not isolated voices. Instruments, unison and crossing parts can mislead. Best with clear unaccompanied singing. Empty tracks mean no reliable pitch estimate, not that a voice is absent.
